Breaking

Behaviours you are removing. Counting beats yes/no — fifty down to ten is progress a checkbox would have hidden.

Extinction is the mechanism: a behaviour fades when its trigger stops paying out. So the count matters more than the streak. A day with three beats a day with twelve, and neither is a broken streak.

Building

Behaviours you are installing. Set a daily target, then log the reps or the minutes.

Acquisition is the opposite mechanism: repetition under a fixed cue. The five-hour blocks live on the Today screen — this tab is for everything smaller than a block.

Who you're becoming

One page, written once, checked on Sundays. Not a journal.

Acting like the person changes who you think you are faster than thinking about it does. That is the only reason this tab exists — so the actions have something to point at.
